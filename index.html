SEISMIC ATTRIBUTES

Advanced Tools for Hydrocarbon Detection & Reservoir Characterization

Seismic attributes transform raw reflection data into a readable map of the subsurface : surfacing faults, fluid fronts, and rock texture invisible to the naked eye. This poster surveys the core attribute families and how they sharpen hydrocarbon detection and reservoir characterization.
